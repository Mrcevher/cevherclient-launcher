OdinMC Kozmetik Kaynak Bilgisi

Animated cosmetic wings (Minecraft Resource Pack), Legeukko
https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/animated-cosmetic-wings
Source version: 1.0.0 (MIT License)
The feather-wing geometry and palette references were converted/adapted from the source pack's player.jem and wings.png into OdinMC's selectable cosmetic mesh format. Slim and regular player bodies are supported by the original asset; OdinMC uses the same centered back placement.
